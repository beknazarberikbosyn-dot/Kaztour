Toilets, water and signal: know before you go

Tourism in our national parks grows faster than their services. Kolsai has no stable mobile signal, and visitors complain about toilets and litter. So every trip now works offline, shows where the facilities are, and lets you tell the park about a problem in one tap.

Offline trip packTicket QR, programme, SOS numbers, a park plan with your GPS position and Kazakh phrases. Opens with no signal.
Facilities mapToilets, bins, water, first aid and where the signal is, with live status from other visitors.
One-tap reports"Bin is full", "No signal here". Saved on your phone and sent when the signal returns.
Park office panelRangers see hotspots, send staff, get a cleaning round and a map of signal gaps for mobile operators.
Safety check-inSay when you will be back. If you are late, your contact gets an SMS with your last point.
Relay by QRNo signal? Pass your reports and last point to a traveler you meet; their phone sends them later.
Facilities from local hostsGuesthouses and cafés near the park open their toilets, showers, water and Wi-Fi. Buy a pass online; it works with no signal.
Host cabinetHosts set prices, open or close a facility, check passes offline and see their income.
